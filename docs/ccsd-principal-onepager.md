# Big Dog Math — Classroom Display Tool

**Steele Wilson · 6th Grade Math · 2026–27**
Requesting review under **CCSD Regulation R-6150** (supplemental instructional materials)

**Live at:** bigdogmath.com

---

## What it is

A website I built that runs the visual side of my classroom. Students see it on the projector;
I drive it from my iPad.

- Lesson displays and worked examples
- Timers and transition screens
- Digital manipulatives (fraction bars, number lines, area models)
- Daily warm-up screens

It supports the CCSD-adopted curriculum. It does not replace it. If it broke tomorrow I'd teach
the same lesson off the board — it's a display tool, not something my instruction depends on.

## What data it collects

**None.**

- No student logins. No student accounts. No sign-in of any kind.
- No student names, email addresses, or ID numbers are entered, collected, or stored.
- No individual student work or answers are displayed on the projector — only whole-class counts,
  like "18 of 24 have an answer" or "the most common wrong answer was 3/4."
- No advertising. Nothing sold or shared. Nothing commercial.

## Content

- Written by me, or drawn from **openly licensed** sources — primarily Illustrative Mathematics /
  Open Up Resources 6–8 Math, which is Creative Commons licensed for exactly this use and carries
  the required attribution.
- No copyrighted publisher material is reproduced on the site. Licensed curriculum is used in class
  as licensed, not republished.
- Aligned to Nevada Academic Content Standards and District curriculum standards.
