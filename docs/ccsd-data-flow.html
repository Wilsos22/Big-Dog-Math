Data safety protocol

  • Encryption. TLS enforced in transit; encrypted at rest by the database provider. Satisfies AUP §K.9.
  • Access control. Row-level security is deny-by-default. Sensitive tables carry no anonymous read policy. Teacher surfaces sit behind authentication.
  • Secret handling. The hashing secret and all database service keys are server-side environment variables only. Never in the client bundle, never in the repository, never in chat.
  • Domain restriction. Sign-in verifies the Google hosted-domain claim matches the district domain. Non-CCSD accounts cannot authenticate.
  • Minimization. The system stores only what instruction requires: a code, a period, and performance signals.
  • Retention. Performance data is scoped to the school year. Codes are rotated at year rollover so a code never follows a student forward. Session records purge after 30 days.
  • Deletion. Any record deleted within 30 days of a request from the District or a parent — the NRS 388.292(3) standard, met voluntarily even though the statute does not attach.
  • No secondary use. No advertising, no targeted advertising, no sale of data, no profiling outside instruction, no third-party analytics.
  • Breach response. Any suspected incident reported to the CCSD Help Desk immediately per AUP §B.5, with affected records identified by code and scope reported same-day.
  • Cost and support burden. $0 to the District. No district integration, no district accounts, no help-desk load.
