# The 90-second demo - script

The one artifact evaluators actually watch. Record at 1080p+, no webcam
needed, your voice over screen capture. Mock class only - no student names
anywhere. Once the /demo mock run-through page exists, shots 4-6 come from
it; until then they come from a real four-device rehearsal with the mock
session (join code MOCKLV).

Rhythm: one sentence per shot. Cut hard between shots. Total 90 seconds.

1. (0:00-0:05) Landing page, cold.
   Say: "This is Big Dog Math - a classroom operating system I built for my
   own 6th-grade math room."

2. (0:05-0:15) /explore, scroll the tool grid slowly.
   Say: "Twenty-four math manipulatives, no login, used by real kids every
   day."

3. (0:15-0:30) One tool, real interaction - /divisibility: answer two rule
   checks, factor pair lands in the family panel.
   Say: "Each tool teaches its own controls and graduates students from
   guided to independent."

4. (0:30-0:50) THE ROOM - the mock run-through: four-up or quick cuts of
   Main projector, Support pace screen, and a student Chromebook advancing
   through warm-up into a poll. End the shot on the teacher tapping Next and
   all three screens moving together.
   Say: "In class, every screen follows one live lesson: the projectors, the
   pace board, and thirty Chromebooks - the teacher advances the room from
   an iPad or phone."

5. (0:50-1:00) The iPad ink: write an equation on the iPad, show it landing
   on the projector in realtime. One bark: the Eyes-up pulse hits the wall
   and the student screen.
   Say: "The iPad is the pen - ink lands on the wall live - and one tap
   calls the room's attention."

6. (1:00-1:15) Mastery view + misconception groups (mock data), then City
   Routes on the remote.
   Say: "Every answer feeds a mastery engine that clusters misconceptions
   live, so the next move is data-informed while the lesson is still
   running."

7. (1:15-1:30) Quick cut: the repo - README top, then scripts/ golden tests
   running green in a terminal.
   Say: "Strict TypeScript, a contract-tested student privacy boundary, and
   the mastery engine golden-tested against a Python oracle - built solo,
   while teaching. bigdogmath.com."

Notes:
- Record shot 5 for real (two devices) - it is the money shot; do not fake it.
- Keep the cursor calm. No dead air. Music optional and quiet.
- Export also a 30-second cut: shots 1, 4, 5, 7.
