# Hook bank

Recurring storylines for lesson hooks. Read this **before** writing any new hook - a
continuing thread beats a fresh one about two times out of three, because it inherits
buy-in already paid for.

Owned by the `lesson-hook` skill. Any agent authoring a lesson adds its row here in the
same turn it writes the hook. A thread that lives only in a Notion lesson is a thread the
next session cannot see.

## Rules

1. Run **three to five threads at a time**. One thread every day becomes a bit, and a bit
   gets old.
2. **Never contradict an established fact.** Abbie is 58 pounds in October; she cannot be
   40 in January without the intervening story being told out loud.
3. **Resolve threads out loud.** An open loop that never closes teaches students the
   stories are fake.
4. **Retire a thread the moment a student can predict it.**
5. Ugly numbers only. 58 pounds and $23.40, never 50 and $20.
6. Steele is never the hero of his own hook - stuck, out of money, losing an argument,
   suspicious, or already wrong.

## Status key

`seed` - drafted, not yet said to students, details still swappable
`open` - live with students, facts are now locked
`resolved` - payoff delivered in class
`retired` - used up, do not reopen

## Thread 1 - Abbie's diet

**Status:** seed
**Families:** A (Steele has a real problem)
**Math it carries:** unit rate, proportion, ratio reasoning, percent change, division

**Established** (nothing is locked until first use - swap for real numbers first)

- Abbie is 58 pounds.
- The vet wants her to drop 4 pounds.
- The food bag says one cup per 20 pounds of dog.
- Steele has been giving her 3 cups a day.

**CONFIRM BEFORE FIRST USE:** Abbie's actual weight and actual food. Students who have
seen her photo will do the eyeball check, and a number that does not match the dog is the
fastest way to teach them the stories are made up. Everything else can stay invented.

**Used in:** none yet

**Next beats**

- The vet weigh-in comes back and she lost the wrong amount - percent change.
- Treats were not counted in the math the first time - the answer they got was wrong for a
  reason they can find.
- Cost per day of the prescription food versus the regular bag - unit rate, comparison.

**Resolution owed:** did she lose the 4 pounds. Do not leave this open past the semester.

## Thread 2 - the passing period fight

**Status:** seed
**Families:** B (the school is doing something)
**Math it carries:** elapsed time, multiplication of large numbers, percent, unit
conversion, estimation

**Established** (swap for whatever your building is actually doing)

- Passing period is dropping from 5 minutes to 4.
- The announcement called it "a negligible change."
- Steele has not decided whether to be annoyed and wants the number first.

**CONFIRM BEFORE FIRST USE:** whether anything real is changing at your school. If nothing
is, use the version where a colleague made the claim rather than the building - a claim
students can check against an adult they do not know is safer than one they might repeat
to an administrator as fact.

**Used in:** none yet

**Next beats**

- Convert the annual figure into something students feel - class periods, school days.
- Flip it: what would the building gain in instructional minutes, and is that trade fair.
- The same math applied to the mile run, lunch, or the fire drill.

**Resolution owed:** state the number out loud and say whether you ended up annoyed.

## Thread 3 - the Friday check split

**Status:** seed
**Families:** D (the disputed claim)
**Math it carries:** order of operations, expressions, division, percent (tip and tax),
equality

**Established**

- Steele splits a check with a friend every Friday.
- The friend says they each owe $23. Steele says $19.
- It has been going on since March, so the gap has compounded into real money.

**CONFIRM BEFORE FIRST USE:** nothing - this one is fully inventable. Keep the friend
unnamed or use a first name students will never meet. Do not name a colleague.

**Used in:** none yet

**Next beats**

- Tip gets added and the disagreement changes shape - percent on top of a split.
- Someone ordered more than someone else, so the even split was never right - ratio.
- The total back-pay owed since March - multiplication, and a satisfying payoff number.

**Resolution owed:** who was wrong. Steele being wrong is the stronger ending and should
be the default unless the mathematics forces otherwise.

## Thread 4 - the 8th grade speed record

**Status:** seed
**Families:** C (invented study / older-kid challenge)
**Math it carries:** mental computation, decomposing a factor, distributive property,
area models, any procedure students think is beneath them

**Established**

- A study timed 8th graders doing mental multiplication.
- Best time was 11 seconds. 34% of them still got it wrong.
- Steele told someone this class could beat that, and may have oversold them.

**CONFIRM BEFORE FIRST USE:** nothing - fully invented and safe. Keep the rival abstract.
The vivid version names the 8th grade teacher next door, but a student will repeat it to
her, so only make that swap if you are comfortable with the conversation.

**Used in:** M1.T1.L1 Taking Apart Numbers and Shapes

**Next beats**

- Raise the bar - the study's 8th graders got a second attempt and improved to 7 seconds.
- Flip the challenge: 8th graders were given a problem this class can now do faster.
- A student beats the record and the class collectively owns the new time.

**Resolution owed:** did the room beat 11 seconds, and by how much. Say the number out
loud the same day.
