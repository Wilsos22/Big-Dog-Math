Handoff notes (draft)

Every screen here is built from the Lesson Steps row for that step: Main Display drives the hero, Pace Directions become the numbered list (line 1 is the action headline), Student Action drives the Chromebook title, Duration drives the clock, Order drives “step N of 12”.

The area model is one component with three inputs — rows, columns, split column — plus a draggable flag. Unit squares are drawn as a CSS grid pattern on the frame with translucent region fills over it, so a 6 × 28 floor costs two elements, not 168. Regions and labels read their color from the addend slot, never from the state accent.

Open questions for the next pass: does the hook need a total suppressed permanently (currently yes, per the step's teacher notes), and should the student model mirror the teacher's drag position live or stay at the authored split?
